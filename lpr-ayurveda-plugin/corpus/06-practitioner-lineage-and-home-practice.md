## 0. How to read this file

- Scope: what leading vaidyas, Kerala institutions, classical pharmacies and educators teach about Amlapitta that rises to the throat (kantha), and the home practice they hand to patients. Classical (T1), institutional (T2) and journal (T3) sources are used to corroborate practitioner (T4) claims.
- Tags: `[T1]` classical text · `[T2]` institutional (hospital/pharmacy monograph, government EDL) · `[T3]` Ayurvedic journal · `[T4]` practitioner/lineage/educator. `[verified x2]` = two independent sources agree · `[single-source]` = one source only · `[variant]` = lineages differ (both positions given) · `[inference]` = compiler's reasoning from sourced principles, not itself sourced · `[VAIDYA]` = the tradition reserves this for a vaidya (rasaushadhi, bhasma, shodhana, snehapana); do not self-start.
- Content rule honoured: no allopathic drugs, pathophysiology or research commentary appear here. Where a source mixed frames, only the Ayurvedic reasoning was kept. The only escalation phrase used is "discuss with your vaidya".
- Doses are as taught in the cited source. They are reference points for a conversation with the vaidya, not a self-prescription, especially for anything tagged `[VAIDYA]`.
- Citations use short keys (e.g. `EA-Amlapitta`) resolved in Section 10.

### 1.4 Synthesis of the treatment logic (as the lineages teach it)

1. **Nidana parivarjana is the medicine.** Every lineage above begins with removing amla, vidahi, viruddha, fermented, curd, late/heavy meals, anger and irregular timing. [verified x2+]
2. **Check for ama before cooling.** If tongue is coated and belching foul (sama pitta/kapha), lineages give deepana-pachana (musta, jeeraka water, dhanyaka, light kanji, langhana) before heavy sweet-cold items like milk, ghee, shatavari, kushmanda. `EA-SamaNirama`, `AB-Ama`, `EA-UdanaSamana` [verified x2]
3. **Then pitta-shamana with madhura-tikta-sheeta**: amalaki, yashtimadhu, shatavari, guduchi, dhanyaka, draksha, dadima, kushmanda, laja, sita, narikela jala, sheeta ghrita/milk. `EA-Amlapitta`, `Sree-Acidity`, `Jiva-Hyper` [verified x2]
4. **Kantha needs kapha-hara at the same time**: the throat is a kapha seat and urdhvaga carries kapha. So the throat regimen is light, warm, non-clogging (kavala, pratimarsha nasya, ela, honey-lukewarm water, warm sips), never cold-heavy. **Charaka Su 4, the ninth mahakashaya (Kanthya), makes kantha rogas tridoshaja with vata-kapha predominance** — so a kantha regimen must not be made **ati-ruksha or ati-sheeta** even though Amlapitta itself is pitta-driven; **goghrita is the classical snigdha counterbalance**, and **yashtimadhu is agrya kanthya** (Charaka Su 25/40). `CSO-Kapha`, `EA-Ch28`, `AH Su 22 via EA-AH22` [verified x2]
5. **Vata-anulomana** (regular meals, early light dinner, triphala, warm oil to body) keeps udana from pushing upward. `EA-Ch28`, `Jiva-Reflux` [verified x2]
6. **Shodhana is the classical cure for the fresh case** (vamana for urdhvaga per Kashyapa; virechana per Kerala practice and AH Sharad rule) and is a vaidya's decision. `[VAIDYA]`
7. **Jirna Amlapitta is yapya**: expect management by rhythm, pathya and periodic review rather than a single cure. `EA-Amlapitta` [verified x2]

## 2. Home practice compendium (gharelu upachara)

Legend for suitability: **P** = pitta-dominant (daha, sour udgara, burning throat) · **K** = kapha-dominant (praseka, throat coating, heaviness, aruchi) · **V** = vata-involved (dry throat, globus, irregular agni, constipation, anxiety). ✓ suits · ~ with care · ✗ avoid.

### 2.1 Cooling and pitta-shamana practices

> **Sheeta qualifier (added 2026-09-18, from Charaka Su 4 Kanthya mahakashaya)**: kantha rogas are tridoshaja with **vata-kapha predominance**. Every cooling item below (cold milk, chilled water, tender coconut, hima, ushira and chandana waters) is therefore taken **cool, never iced, and never ati-ruksha**, and is balanced by a snigdha item — classically **goghrita** — whenever the throat is dry, coated or hoarse. Pitta-shamana for the amashaya must not be bought at the price of a chilled, dried throat.

## 8. Questions to bring to the vaidya

1. **Prakriti** — am I pitta-, kapha- or vata-led by constitution? (Case reports show vata-kapha prakriti can carry a pitta vikriti, changing the whole plan.) `JAIMS-Case`
2. **Vikriti now** — which dosha dominates today's throat picture: daha (pitta), praseka/coating (kapha), dryness/globus (vata)? Is it sama (ama present) or nirama? `AB-Ama`
3. **Agni** — vishama, tikshna, manda or sama? Should deepana-pachana precede cooling? `EA-UdanaSamana`
4. **Koshtha** — krura, madhya or mridu: this fixes the dose of Avipattikara/Triphala and whether monthly virechana-lite is safe. `JAIMS-Case`, `EA-Avipattikar`
5. **Satmya** — which of the home items are habitual and safe for me (milk, takra, coconut, amla juice)? Which are asatmya?
6. **Bala and season** — is shodhana indicated at all this year, and which: vamana (Kashyapa order for urdhvaga) or virechana (Kerala practice / Sharad rule)? When? `IJAM-Vamana`, `Sree-Acidity`, `EA-Ritu`
7. **Formulation review** — of the current kashayam/gulika/lehyam/ghrita layers, which is for agni, which for pitta, which for kantha; any rasaushadhi or bhasma (Kamadudha, Sutashekhara, Shankha) and their course length? `AVS-Reflux`, `AMI-*`
8. **Anupana** — for each medicine: warm water, milk, sita-jiraka, amalaki-ghee-sugar, honey? `AMI-Kamdudha`, `JAIMS-Kamadudha`
9. **Duration and taper** — how many weeks per layer; what is capped at 2 months (Avipattikara, Kamadudha)? `EA-Avipattikar`, `AMI-Kamadugha`
10. **Throat regimen** — which gandusha liquid (tila taila vs ghee/milk vs triphala), which nasya oil (Anu vs ghee), and is dhumapana ever appropriate for me? `EA-AH22`, `EA-Nasya`, `PA-ChSu5`
11. **Pathya list in writing** — grains, pulses, vegetables, fruits, dairy rules (milk yes/no, takra when), spices allowed, cooking fat, water temperature. `JAIMS-Pathya`, `AVS-Reflux`
12. **Fasting rule** — may I do a weekly light day; what may I take on it (kanji, laja manda, fruit)? `EA-Fasting`
13. **Voice** — how much speaking is safe; when to rest; Bhramari/Om dosage. `EA-SU53`, `Sree-Acidity`
14. **Rasayana** — when (after which shodhana, which month) and which (Amalaki, Kushmanda, Shatavari, Narikela khanda)? `EA-Amlapitta`
15. **Monthly review markers** — which of Section 7's signs the vaidya wants recorded (tongue, udgara, hunger time, stool, sleep, kantha score).
