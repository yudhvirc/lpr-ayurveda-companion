T2 `[single-source]`
- Institutional pattern in Amlapitta series: Pitta-Kapha prakriti most common (54% Jamnagar 2013; 47% Tirupati 2017); Krura koshtha in about 90% (Jamnagar 2013); Mandagni or Vishamagni in most; Rajasika manas in about 60–70%. T3 `[verified x2]`
- Daha is graded by *sites* — Ura, Udara, Kukshi, **Kantha** — and by duration/relief: grade 1 = one site occasionally more than half an hour; grade 2 = two sites daily half to one hour; grade 3 = more than two sites daily for an hour or more, relieved by digestion or vomiting; grade 4 = most sites, sleep disturbed. This is the IPGT&RA Jamnagar grading reproduced in IJAM 2023 (Thakare) and IJHM 2025 (Hamand). T3 `[verified x2]` — useful language for describing throat burning to a vaidya.

## 2. NAMASTE portal, NAMC morbidity codes and standard terms

### 2.1 The system (T2, `[verified x2]`: Thrigulla & Narayanam, JAIM 2023;14(4):100781; JRAS 2024 NAMASTE evaluation; Ministry of Ayush portal)
- **NAMASTE** = National Ayush Morbidity and Standardized Terminologies Electronic portal, Ministry of Ayush, developed by CCRAS with NIIMH Hyderabad, IPGT&RA Jamnagar and NIA Jaipur; launched 17 Oct 2017 (Ayurveda Day) at AIIA New Delhi. Portal: https://namaste.ayush.gov.in (older host namstp.ayush.gov.in).
- **NAMC (National Ayurveda Morbidity Codes)** = 2,895 coded disease entities (SAT-E), organised alphabetically into groups A–W by six diagnostic frames: dosha/dhatu/mala level; organ/srotas-based; causative-factor groups; epidemiological (infection/infestation); constitutional/health status; treatment-outcome descriptors.
- **SAT (Standardized Ayurveda Terminologies)** = 14,753 terms: SAT-A fundamentals (318), SAT-B sharira (300), SAT-C nidana/vyadhi-vinischaya (175), **SAT-D linga/lakshana — 9,400 lakshanas (signs and features)**, SAT-E = NAMC, SAT-F dravya (565), SAT-G aushadha kalpana (197), SAT-H ahara kalpana (116), SAT-I chikitsa (663), SAT-J svasthavritta (124). Each entry carries Term ID, Parent ID, code, term in Devanagari plus transliteration, short and long definition, classical reference. Downloadable as MS-Excel (SAT-A…J and SAT-COMBINED) from https://namaste.ayush.gov.in/sat_Ayurveda.
- Dual recording: the NAMC entry for an Ayurvedic vyadhi can also be carried in the ICD-11 Traditional Medicine module, which exists precisely so that a diagnosis such as Amlapitta keeps its own Ayurvedic identity in an institutional record rather than being re-described in another system. It is a filing device for Ayurvedic terms, not a diagnosis in itself. BIS glossaries IS 17424 Parts 1–5 (2020–21) are derived from SAT.
- Real-world use: Amlapitta is among the ten vyadhis most often recorded under NAMC in Ayush institutions, and both Amlapitta and Ajirna entries rise in the warmer part of the year, which matches the classical Pitta-prakopa kala — JRAS 2024 (CCRAS journal). T2/T3 `[single-source]`.

Format for each entry: **journal, year, citation/URL** — presentation — nidana — protocol — Ayurvedic outcome — follow-up — throat note. Outcomes are given only as lakshana relief (complete / marked / moderate / mild). ★ marks entries that explicitly record throat lakshanas (Kantha daha, Kantha vidaha, Gala, Swarabheda, Kasa, Praseka, Kapha-nishthivana).

### 4.1 Government-institute series (T2 institutions, T3 journals on PubMed Central)

**C1 ★ AYU 2012;33(2):219–223 — Baragi UC, Vyas MK (IPGT&RA, Gujarat Ayurved University, Jamnagar). doi:10.4103/0974-8520.105241; PMC3611627.**
Presentation: 138 patients of *Urdhwaga Amlapitta*; Amla-tikta udgara and **Hrit-kantha daha present in every patient**; Avipaka, Udara shula, Chhardi, Aruchi, Hrillasa, Gaurava, Agnimandya, Vibandha, Shirah-shula common. Dosha: Pitta with Kapha; Rasa dhatu dushti; Krura koshtha in 28% (Vibandha 78%). Nidana: Katu, Amla, Lavana ahara; tobacco chewing; Adhyashana; disturbed sleep; stress. Protocol: Dashanga Kwatha Ghana Vati (Chakradatta 52/11; Tikta–Kashaya rasa, Katu–Madhura vipaka, Sheeta virya, Pitta-Kapha shamaka) two ghana vati tablets of 250 mg each, twice daily after food with water, 8 weeks; diet unchanged except **strict restriction of spicy food**. Reasoning: Tikta converts Sama Pitta to Nirama, kindles Agni, prevents Annavisha. Outcome: marked relief in Amla-tikta udgara, **Hrit-kantha daha (about four-fifths relief)**, Udara shula, Avipaka; Chhardi almost fully relieved; most patients "marked improvement". Follow-up: weekly during treatment, then 4 weeks.

**C2 ★ AYU 2013;34(4):352–355 — Baragi UC, Vyas MK (Jamnagar). doi:10.4103/0974-8520.127705; PMC3968695.**
Same 138 patients, aetiology study. **Hrit-kantha daha 100%**, Avipaka 99%, Hrillasa 96%, Udara shula 91%, Gaurava 91%, Agnimandya 80%. Nidana ranking: Katu 99%, Amla 96%, Guru 91%, Snigdha 86%, Viruddha 82%, Abhishyandi 82%, Ati-ushna 74%, Diwaswapna 90%, Antarodaka-pana 82%, Chinta 80%. Prakriti: Pitta-Kapha 54%; Rajasika 69%; **Krura koshtha 90%**. Samprapti: improper diet → Agni impairment → Vidagdha anna → Amlapitta. Use: the nidana checklist for the patient's own diary.

**C3 ★ AYU 2011;32(3):361–364 — Kumar J, Dave AR, Vyas MG (Jamnagar). doi:10.4103/0974-8520.93915; PMC3326883.**
41 patients; chief complaints Kukshi daha, Hrid daha, **Kantha daha**, Amla/Tikta udgara, Avipaka, Gaurava, Shirah-shula, Klama. Samprapti: Vidagdha Pitta with Drava and Amla guna. Protocol: one set of the 41 received Shatapatrayadi churna tablet (Bheshaja Samhita), the other Patoladi yoga tablet (Bhaishajya Ratnavali: Patola, Guduchi, Shunthi, Katuki); 1 month; weekly review. Outcome assessed as Rogabala, Agnibala, Dehabala, Chetasabala: Shatapatrayadi gave about three-quarters overall relief, Patoladi about two-thirds; both relieved Kantha daha.

**C4 ★ AYU 2015;36(2):157–162 — Prajapati SM, Patel BR (Dravyaguna, IPGT&RA Jamnagar). doi:10.4103/0974-8520.175551; PMC4784125.**
40 patients of Amlapitta (Madhava criteria). Protocol: **Yashtimadhu mula churna 2 g thrice daily with water for 2 weeks**, with a second set of patients given Jethimala (Taverniera nummularia) mula churna, the regional substitute, so the two dravyas could be described side by side. Rationale: Madhura rasa, Guru–Snigdha, Sheeta virya, Madhura vipaka; Pitta-Vata shamaka, Dahashamaka (Bhavaprakasha). Outcome: about two-thirds relief in Tikta-amla udgara, **Hrit-kantha daha** and Utklesha within 2 weeks; half of completers "marked improvement". Note: the standard Yashtimadhu dose for a throat-burning Pitta picture in a government institute.

**C5 ★ AYU 2017;38(1-2):15–23 — Ghosh K, Baghel MS (Kayachikitsa, IPGT&RA Jamnagar). doi:10.4103/ayu.AYU_75_14; PMC5954255.**
112 patients typed by Kashyapa's doshic lakshanas (Kaphaja: Aruchi, Gaurava, Jadyata, **Kapha-nishthivana**, Lepa, Sada, Sheeta, Vami, Kandu; Pittaja: Trishna, **Hrit-kantha-kukshi daha**, Murchha, Bhrama, Moha, Hrillasa, Sweda, Anga-pitata). Nidana: Ati-guru bhojana 96%, Vidahi 85%, Viruddha 83%, Katu 80%, Bhuktamatra-swapna 73%, tea 97%, tobacco 52%, Vegadharana 53%, Prajagarana 40%, Krodha 54%. Dosha frequency: Kapha 56%, Pitta 47%, Vata 33%; Purishavaha and Rasavaha srotas most involved. Protocol: Kapha-dominant → **Shunthi Khanda 5 g twice daily between meals with water, 30 days**; Pitta-dominant → **Vasa-khanda Kushmandaka 5 g twice daily, 30 days**; a further set of patients, selected only by the cardinal lakshanas, received the same two khandas without dosha matching. Outcome: Hrit-kantha daha, Utklesha, Tikta-amla udgara, Gaurava, Chhardi, Shula all markedly relieved; those whose khanda was chosen by doshic predominance eased more fully, and their lakshanas returned less often at the 1-week review, than those treated by cardinal lakshanas alone; Dehabala items improved including **Swara-varna yoga (voice and complexion)**. Principle, as the authors state it: treatment chosen by doshic predominance eases the disease more fully than treatment chosen by the cardinal lakshanas alone.

**C6 AYU 2011;32(4):512–517 — Pol H, More S, Vyas MK, Vyas H, Ravishankar B (Jamnagar; SDM Udupi). doi:10.4103/0974-8520.96125; PMC3361927.**
*Drava-bahula Amlapitta* (Pitta with dominant liquid quality): Chhardi, Kandu/Kotha, Angasada, Amlodgara, Utklesha, Klama, Avipaka, Guru-koshthata, Aruchi, Vidbheda. Nidana: Snigdha–Ushna–Guru–Drava diet 69%, Samashana/Vishamashana/Viruddhashana 81%, Guru bhojana 83%; Avara jarana shakti 88%. Protocol: **Bhringaraja tablet 500 mg × 4 twice daily, 4 weeks** — Ruksha and Ushna guna chosen deliberately to oppose Drava-bahula Pitta (the "fundamental approach": treat the aggravated guna). Pathya: classical pathyahara. Outcome: Chhardi fully relieved; Amlodgara, Utklesha, Klama, Avipaka about four-fifths relieved.

**C7 ★ AYU 2017;38(3-4):133–138 — Kumar HK, Sridurga CH, Rao DB (SV Ayurvedic College, Tirupati). doi:10.4103/ayu.AYU_201_17; PMC6153916.**
30 patients; Amlodgara, Daha, Gaurava, Utklesha, Avipaka, Agnimandya. Prakriti Pitta-Kapha 47%; Agni: Manda 26%, Tikshna 26%, Vishama 24%; Mridu koshtha 60%. **C8 ★ AYU 2010;31(4):482–486 — Rajashekhara N, Sharma PP (KVG Ayurveda College, Sullia). doi:10.4103/0974-8520.82047; PMC3202266.**

**C9 ★ AYU 2011;32(4):494–499 — Acharya S et al. doi:10.4103/0974-8520.96122; PMC3361924.**
30 patients (20–35 y) with Avipaka, **Hrit-kantha daha**, Tikta-amlodgara, Utklesha, Udara shula, Adhmana, Aruchi. Protocol: Chincha kshara or Kadali kshara 500 mg thrice daily with water, 30 days. Outcome: Chincha kshara cured 4 of 10 and gave maximum improvement in 4 more; Kadali kshara cured 3, maximum improvement 5; relief in chest-and-throat burning, sour eructation, nausea, bloating; no untoward effects.

**C10 ★ JAIM 2021;12(1):87–92 — Khapre M, Nautiyal V, Jagzape M, Saxena V (Dept of Ayush, AIIMS Rishikesh). doi:10.1016/j.jaim.2020.12.004; PMC8039346.**
30 patients of Urdhwaga Amlapitta; Tikta-amlodgara and Guru-koshthata in 93%, Avipaka, Gaurava, Aruchi, Vibandha, Shiroruja, Utklesha (Hrit-kantha daha and Klama were present in the patients but were not among the lakshanas the authors followed visit by visit). Nidana: Amla, Katu, Lavana, Guru, Snigdha diet; stress; tobacco; alcohol. Outcome: relief reported from the second visit (day 7); **all lakshanas relieved by day 21**; almost all patients cured by day 28; Kapha-prakriti patients improved faster once Ama was digested, Pitta-prakriti patients only after the full Sheeta combination was started. Follow-up weekly; phone follow-up if a visit was missed.

### 4.2 Panchakarma case reports and series

**C13 ★ IJAM 2017;8(1):41–44 — Thakur R, Wetal V (Panchakarma, Sumatibhai Shah Ayurved Mahavidyalaya, Hadapsar, Pune). doi:10.47552/ijam.v8i1.920.**
29-year-old man, 2 years of Pitta-udirana, Chhardi, Amlodgara, **Hrit-kantha daha**, Avipaka, Ajirna; jirna avastha; diagnosed Urdhvaga Amlapitta (Bhavaprakasha: coloured, Kapha-mixed vomiting). Protocol: Deepana–Pachana **Hingvashtaka churna 1 g thrice daily before food with warm water, 3 days**; Snehapana **Kantakari ghrita 60 ml increasing 10 ml/day for 5 days** until samyak snigdha lakshanas; diet during snehapana drava, ushna, anabhishyandi; abhyanga + swedana with tila taila for 2 days; **Vamana**: Akantha-pana with cow milk, Vamana dravya Madanaphala 2 g + Yashtimadhu 2 g + Vacha 1 g + Saindhava 2 g licked with honey; Vamanopaga Yashtimadhu kwatha after each vega until Pittanta; 6 vegas, Madhyama shuddhi; Dhumapana; Peyadi samsarjana krama 5 days. Outcome: **all lakshanas absent on day 15 (upashaya)**; no recurrence at 6, 12 and 18 months. Reasoning: Vamana removes Kapha-Pitta from Amashaya, the seat of the disease, so recurrence is prevented.

**C14 ★ IJMER 2021;10(11-6):28–32 — Panchal KD, Nakade MM, Ganbote PD (Panchakarma, Dr DY Patil College of Ayurveda, Pimpri, Pune). doi:10.11.106 (ijmer).**

### 5.3 What the pattern implies for a Urdhvaga picture with throat lakshanas (synthesis, not a prescription — discuss with your vaidya)
1. Institutions treat **Hrit-kantha daha as a Pitta-Drava-guna sign**: Sheeta–Madhura–Kshariya shamana (Kamadudha, Shankha, Pravala, Yashtimadhu with milk, Kushmanda, Shatavari/Narayana ghrita) recurs whenever throat burning is the leading lakshana.
2. When Aruchi, Gaurava, coated tongue, **Kapha-nishthivana** or Tiktasyata accompany it (Kapha-anubandha), Tikta–Katu deepana (Patoladi, Dashanga, Chhinnodbhavadi, Hingvashtaka, Shunthi khanda, Chitrakadi) is given first or alongside, then withdrawn as Ama clears (C10, C25).
3. Chronic (>1 year) Urdhvaga cases with coloured/bitter regurgitation were sent for **Vamana** in every Panchakarma paper, with Yashtimadhu kwatha as the emetic adjuvant and no recurrence recorded over 12–18 months (C13, C14).
4. Voice and throat-specific adjuncts come from the Kasa/Swarabheda literature, not from the Amlapitta series: Kanthya dravyas (Yashtimadhu, Draksha, Pippali, Sariva), Gandusha with Yashtimadhu–Triphala–Haridra–Saindhava, Khadiradi/Lavangadi/Eladi gutika, Marsha nasya, Kalyana avaleha, and Madhura-Sheeta ghrita for Pittaja Svarabheda (C42–C46).
