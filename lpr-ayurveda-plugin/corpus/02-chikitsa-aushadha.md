# 02 — Chikitsa and Aushadha: treatment principles, dravyas, yogas, kantha therapies, panchakarma, rasayana

Scope: Ayurvedic treatment of Amlapitta with emphasis on Urdhvaga Amlapitta (upward-moving) reaching the kantha (throat) and svara (voice). Frame: Amlapitta is Pitta whose natural katu (pungent) quality has turned amla (sour) through mandagni (weak digestive fire) and ama (undigested residue) in the amashaya, seat of Kapha and Pitta; Udana and Samana vayu carry the vidagdha (fermented) pitta-kapha upward to hrit-kantha (chest-throat). Terms in simple transliteration with English gloss on first use. Tagging per CORPUS-RULES.md. Items marked **VAIDYA** are vaidya-prescribed in the tradition itself. Compiled 2026-09-17.

Escalation phrase used in this file: "discuss with your vaidya".

## 1. Chikitsa sutra — treatment principles for Amlapitta

### 1.1 The three-part structure
- Nidana parivarjana (removing causes) → shodhana (elimination) → shamana (pacification), with pathya (wholesome regimen) throughout. Sushruta names nidana parivarjana the first line of all treatment; the reviews of Amlapitta apply it explicitly, since continued nidana keeps producing mandagni and amla-drava pitta [T1: Sushruta Uttara 1/25, as cited; T3: IJMHS 2022 "A classical quick review of Amlapitta", Chikitsa section] [verified x2].
- Kashyapa gives the fullest protocol: vamana (therapeutic emesis) first for one whose strength and bulk are intact; then langhana (lightening/fasting) and laghu bhojana (light meals); then shamana-pachana aushadhi; then sramsana (mild purgation) once doshas ripen and descend; then pathya; then, if all fails, change of place (desha parivartana) away from anupa (marshy, humid) land [T1: Kashyapa Samhita Khila Sthana 16/18-19 (vamana), 16/20-21 (langhana, laghu bhojana, shamana-pachana), 16/24-25 (pachana yogas), 16/30 (sramsana), 16/38-41 (pathya), 16/44-45 (desha parivartana), Hemraj Sharma ed. with Vidyotini, read first-hand 2026-09-18; T3: IJAM 2016;7(1):1-5 Kuriakose et al.] [verified x2].

### 1.2 The Kashyapa shlokas the patient should know
- Seat of the disease: "vyādhir āmāśayotthoʼyaṃ kaphapitte tadāśraye" — the disease arises from the amashaya, the seat of Kapha and Pitta; therefore emesis from that very seat is the root treatment, "like felling a tree by cutting its roots" [T1: Kashyapa Khila 16/18-19 — "vyādhir āmāśayotthoʼyaṁ kaphapittē tadāśrayē | tasmādādita ēvāsya mūlacchēdāya buddhimān || akṣīṇabalamāṁsasya vamanaṁ saṁprakalpayēt | nānyō mānyaḥ kramō hyasya śāntayē vamanāṛtē" — Hemraj Sharma ed., read first-hand 2026-09-18; the earlier 16/22-23 attribution from the IJAM appraisal is superseded] [verified x2].
- Vamana dravyas: "lavaṇāmbunā sukhoṣṇena kṣīreṇekṣurasena vā / madhūdakena tiktair vā vamanaṃ saṃprakalpayet" — induce emesis with salt water, comfortably warm milk, sugarcane juice, honey-water or bitter drugs [T1: Kashyapa Khila 16/31, read first-hand 2026-09-18 — the JAIMS numbering is correct and the IJAM "16/32-37" is superseded; 16/32-33 is the Triphala-Trayamana-Katuka-Rohini-Trivrit virechana churna, not the vamana media] [verified x2].
- Why no other liquid medicine while doshas are utklishta (excited, moving upward): they will not be digested because agni is weak; only emesis is proper at that moment — "dōṣōtklēśē na sahasā dravam auṣadhamācarēt | vamanīyāhṛtē tad dhi na samyak paripacyatē" [T1: Kashyapa Khila 16/22, read first-hand 2026-09-18; 16/23 adds that once things have matured the drava aushadhi does shamana, pachana and bhedana; T3: IJAM 2016] [verified x2].
- After vamana, residual doshas are settled by langhana, laghu bhojana and shamana-pachana yogas suited to satmya (what is habituated) and kala (time) — "tasyōpaśamanaṁ kuryāllagghanairlaghubhōjanaiḥ | sātmyakālōpapannaiśca yōgaiḥ śamanapācanaiḥ" [T1: Kashyapa Khila 16/20-21, read first-hand 2026-09-18; T3: IJMHS 2022] [verified x2].
- When strength has returned, the doshas that have reached the pakvashaya (lower gut) are removed by sramsana (gentle purgation) [T1: Kashyapa Khila 16/30, read first-hand 2026-09-18] [single-source].
- Dosha-wise upashaya (relief): Vataja → snigdha (unctuous) regimen; Pittaja → svadu-sheeta (sweet-cold) regimen; Kaphaja → ruksha-ushna (dry-warm) regimen [T1: Kashyapa Khila 16/16-17, read first-hand 2026-09-18 — Vata: shula, angasada, jrimbha, snigdhopashayita; Pitta: dhuma, vidaha, svadu-sheeta-upashayita; Kapha: gurutva, chardi, ruksha-ushna-upashayita; T3: IJAM 2016; IJMHS 2022] [verified x2].
- Rasa sequence for Pitta: "tikta-svādu-kaṣāyāḥ syuḥ kramaśaḥ paittike hitāḥ" — bitter, then sweet, then astringent, in that order [T1: Kashyapa Khila 6/27 as quoted by JAIMS] [single-source].
- Pathya and conduct close the chapter: purana shali, mudga, masura, harenu, goghrita, milk, jangala mamsa, kalaya shaka, Poutika (Karanja), **Vasa pushpa**, Vastuka and other tikta-laghu, avidahi vegetables (16/38-40); lashuna, Haritaki, Pippali, ghrita and well-aged madya to raise agni and bala, with the stated nidanas given up (16/41); then "yuktāhāravihārasya yuktavyāyāmasēvinaḥ | śuktakōʼyamalōlasya śāmyatyātmavataḥ sataḥ" — proper diet and living, befitting exercise, freedom from tongue-greed (alola), self-command (atmavan) and being a good man make the disease subside (16/42) [T1: Kashyapa Khila 16/38-42, read first-hand 2026-09-18] [verified x2].

### 1.3 Madhava / Chakradatta / Bhavaprakasha / Yogaratnakara / Bhaishajya Ratnavali — the direction rule
- Madhava divides by dosha-gati: Urdhvaga (upward: chardi/vomiting of sour-bitter material, kantha-hrit-kukshi daha, shiroruja, udgara) and Adhoga (downward) [T1: Madhava Nidana 51/3-6] [verified x2 with easyayurveda translation].
- Bhavaprakasha and Yogaratnakara state the rule that governs this corpus: **Urdhvaga Amlapitta → Vamana; Adhoga Amlapitta → Virechana** [T1: Bhavaprakasha Madhyama Khanda, Amlapitta adhikara; Yogaratnakara Amlapitta chikitsa; T3: IJMHS 2022 Upashaya table; IJNRD 2023 review] [verified x2].
- Chakrapani's dosha-seat logic (Madhukosha commentary line reproduced in reviews): dosha produced in amashaya → vamana; in pachyamanashaya → virechana; in pakvashaya → basti; Yogaratnakara adds raktamokshana for stubborn cases; Chakrapani, Vrinda Madhava and Govinda Das describe niruha basti [T1: Madhava Nidana 51 with Madhukosha; Chakradatta Amlapitta chikitsa; T3: IJNRD 2023 review] [single-source for the basti line].
- Shamana qualities: snigdha guna, sheeta virya, madhura and tikta rasa are the pacifying properties; tikta dravyas dry the drava (liquid) quality of vitiated pitta (Vayu mahabhuta) and clear srotorodha (channel obstruction, Akasha mahabhuta) [T1: Sharangadhara Madhyama Khanda per IJNRD; T3: IJAM 2016 Panchamahabhuta interpretation] [verified x2].
- Deepana-pachana without aggravating Pitta: Kashyapa's own pachana pairs are tikta-dominant with only small ushna (hot) support — Nagara + Ativisha + Musta; Nagara + Ativisha + Abhaya (Haritaki); Trayamana + Patola-patra + Katukarohini — taken alone or in Kiratatikta/Katuki decoction "until the amashaya is clear", after which agni normalises and utsaha, tushti, pushti, vapu-bala (energy, contentment, nourishment, body-strength) return [T1: Kashyapa Khila 16/24-25 (the three pachana pairs and the Kiratatikta / Rohini kwatha), 16/26-29 (agni-samadhana and its fruit), read first-hand 2026-09-18] [verified x2].
- Langhana rule: fast or eat lightly for the ama phase; langhana precedes drava (liquid) aushadhi because a weak agni cannot digest medicine [T1: Kashyapa Khila 16/20-22, read first-hand 2026-09-18; T3: Ayurlog 2021 review] [verified x2].
- Bhavaprakasha's teaching "roga sarve api mande agnau" (all disease from weak agni) is the reason deepana-pachana is never skipped even when symptoms are hot [T1: Ashtanga Hridaya Nidana 12/1 as cited in IJMHS 2022] [single-source].

### 1.4 Shodhana vs shamana — decision logic (as the texts frame it)
| Situation | Indicated | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Bala (strength) and mamsa intact, doshas utklishta and upward-moving, kantha-hrit daha, sour-bitter chardi/udgara | Vamana first; no other drava aushadhi until emesis done | [T1: Kashyapa Khila 16/18-19 (vamana for the one whose bala and mamsa are unimpaired) and 16/22 (no drava aushadhi while doshas are utklishta), read first-hand 2026-09-18; Bhavaprakasha] [verified x2] |
| Kapha-dominant (guru, aruchi, praseka, kaphanishthivana) with Urdhvaga | Vamana; then ruksha-ushna-laghu regimen | [T1: Kashyapa 16/16-17, 16/18-19; Yogaratnakara] [verified x2] |
| Pitta-dominant / Adhoga (daha, trishna, bhrama, vidbheda) | Mridu virechana (mild purgation) with Trivrit + madhura/tikta anupana, Avipattikara | [T1: Bhavaprakasha; Chakradatta; Kashyapa 16/32-33 (Triphala-Trayamana-Katuka-Rohini-Trivrit churna)] [verified x2] |
| Vata involvement (shoola, adhmana, antrakujana, snigdhopashaya) | Basti (niruha with tikta-madhura kwatha; anuvasana with medicated ghee) | [T1: Chakradatta / Vrinda Madhava per IJNRD] [single-source] |
| Weak, elderly, emaciated, krisha, or during active fever | Shamana only; shodhana deferred | [T1: general rule Charaka Siddhi 2 (vamana-virechana anarha)] [single-source] |
| Jirna (chronic) Amlapitta after shodhana | Rasayana | [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 1/1/24-28 rasayana adhikari after shodhana] [verified x2 with easyayurveda translation] |

Order of treatment (composite): nidana parivarjana → langhana/laghu bhojana → deepana-pachana (tikta-pradhana) → vamana (Urdhvaga) or virechana (Adhoga) with purva-paschat karma → samsarjana krama → shamana yogas (tikta-madhura-sheeta) → basti if Vata persists → rasayana → achara rasayana [T1: Kashyapa Khila 16; Charaka Chikitsa 1] [verified x2].

Prognosis reminder that shapes dosing: nava (recent) Amlapitta is sadhya (curable), jirna is yapya (manageable); with upadravas (jvara, atisara, pandu, shoola, shotha, aruchi, bhrama) and dhatu-kshaya it is not cured [T1: Madhava Nidana 51/7; Kashyapa Khila 16/49 — seven upadravas named: jvara, atisara, pandutva, shula, shotha, aruchi, bhrama, with kshina-dhatu; read first-hand 2026-09-18] [verified x2].

Doses are API/AFI ranges for churna unless stated; kwatha (decoction) dose is Sharangadhara 2 pala ≈ 48-96 ml [T1: Sharangadhara Madhyama 2; T4: easyayurveda churna kalpana] [verified x2]. ## 4. Kantha-specific chikitsa — the kantha emphasis in Urdhvaga Amlapitta

### 4.1 Kavala and gandusha (gargle and mouth-hold)
- Definitions: gandusha = a quantity that cannot be rolled about in the mouth (the mouth filled to capacity and held without movement); kavala = a quantity that can be easily and conveniently rolled about, i.e. gargled [T1: Sushruta Chikitsa 40/42 (Dhuma-Nasya-Kavalagraha chikitsita), read first-hand; Ashtanga Hridaya Sutra 22/11] [verified x2].
- Four types (AH Sutra 22/1): **Snigdha/Snehana** (oil with madhura-amla-lavana dravyas; Vata), **Shamana** (tikta-kashaya-madhura; **Pitta** — the type for Urdhvaga Amlapitta throat), **Shodhana** (tikta-katu-amla-lavana, ushna; Kapha), **Ropana** (kashaya-tikta; healing a raw, vrana-like kantha) [T1: AH Sutra 22/1-4] [verified x2 with easyayurveda and slideshare renderings].
- Duration: hold until the dosha (kapha) gathers in the cheeks and throat and secretions run copiously from nose and eyes; then spit it out, take a fresh mouthful, and sit erect with an undistracted mind while holding it [T1: Sushruta Chikitsa 40/43, read first-hand; AH Sutra 22/11] [verified x2].
- Kavala benefits list includes kantha (throat) disorders, dryness, nausea, drowsiness, loss of appetite, and diseases of head, ear, mouth, eye [T1: AH Sutra 22/12] [single-source].
- Tila taila gandusha (daily): "strength to jaws, **voice**, plumpness of face, taste, no dryness of throat (kantha-shosha), no chapping of lips, no dental decay" [T1: Charaka Sutra 5/78-80] [verified x2].
- Honey gandusha: cleans, quickly heals mukhapaka (mouth sores), relieves daha and trishna; Sushruta gives tila, Nilotpala, ghrita, sugar, milk and honey as the gandusha for burning inside the mouth [T1: AH Sutra 22/7; Sushruta Chikitsa 40/46, read first-hand] [verified x2].
- Liquids for this patient, in Ayurvedic order of choice:
  1. **Yashtimadhu kwatha**, lukewarm (Shamana-Ropana) — madhura-sheeta; the JNR svarabheda case used Yashtimadhu + Triphala + Saindhava + Haridra gandusha for 7 days [T3: J Nat Remedies svarabheda case; T1: AH Sutra 22 Shamana type] [verified x2].
  2. **Tila taila** (Snigdha), 10-15 ml held 5-10 minutes in the morning after brushing — for kantha-shosha, svara-bala; skip when kapha is thick and sticky [T1: Charaka Sutra 5/78-80] [single-source for the exclusion].
  3. **Triphala kwatha** (Shodhana-Ropana) — when kapha coats the throat (praseka, sticky sputum); kashaya may dry a pitta throat, so alternate with Yashti. Sushruta’s atiyoga (excess) signs are the check — trishna (thirst), aruchi (aversion to food), mukha-shosha (dry mouth), klama (fatigue), mukhapaka (inflamed mouth); samyak yoga is relief of the disorder, lightness and purity of the mouth, a cheerful mind and clear senses [T1: Sushruta Chikitsa 40/44-45, read first-hand; AH Sutra 22 Shodhana/Ropana types] [verified x2].
  4. **Khadira kwatha** (Ropana) — for a raw (vrana-like), foul-smelling throat; Khadiradi vati is its tablet form [T1: AH Sutra 22 Ropana; BR Khadiradi] [verified x2].
  5. **Warm water with a pinch of Saindhava** — simple Shodhana kavala for morning kapha; keep salt minimal since lavana atiyoga is itself a nidana (Sushruta's "Amlika") [T1: Sushruta Sutra 42 lavana atiyoga per IJAM review] [single-source].
  6. **Ghrita or Yashtimadhu ghrita** held in the mouth — Snigdha-Shamana for a raw, burning kantha and svara after an acute urdhvaga vega [T1: AH Chikitsa 3 Yashtimadhu ghrita; AH Sutra 22 Snigdha type] [single-source].
- Timing: after teeth cleaning, before food; never immediately after a meal or when nausea (utklesha) is present, because the fluid stimulates more kapha [T1: AH Sutra 22 sequence in dinacharya] [single-source].

### 4.2 Nasya (nasal medication)
- Types (AH Sutra 20): Virechana (shodhana), Brimhana, Shamana; by dose, Marsha (higher) and Pratimarsha (2 drops) [T1: AH Sutra 20/1-3, 20/28-30] [verified x2].
- Pratimarsha nasya, 2 drops per nostril, is the only nasya meant for daily self-use; timings include after waking, after brushing, after gandusha, after exercise, evening [T1: Sushruta Chikitsa 40/51; AH Sutra 20] [verified x2].
- **Contraindications that bear on Amlapitta**: soon after food (bhuktabhakta), **ajirna (indigestion)**, immediately after ghee/oil intake, thirst, hunger, fatigue, after vamana or virechana on the same day, nava jvara, nava pratishyaya, intoxication, cloudy or unseasonal days [T1: Charaka Siddhi 2; AH Sutra 20; T4: easyayurveda nasya page] [verified x2].
- Applied rule: **no nasya while Urdhvaga Amlapitta is active with utklesha, chardi, ama (aruchi, guru koshtha, tikta-amla udgara) or ajirna**; resume pratimarsha only in the nirama phase, mid-morning, 1-2 hours away from food [T1: AH Sutra 20 contraindication list; Kashyapa 16/22 (no other drava aushadhi while doshas are utklishta)] [verified x2 by inference].
- Oil choice: Anu taila (Charaka Sutra 5/56-63) is the dinacharya oil that "strengthens the senses, hair, and prevents urdhvajatrugata roga"; for pitta-dominant throats vaidyas use Ksheerabala taila or Chandanadi taila as pratimarsha at midday [T1: Charaka Sutra 5/56-71; T4: easyayurveda nasya page] [verified x2].
- Season: Anu taila nasya is for pravrit, sharad, vasanta (pre-rains, autumn, spring) when the sky is clear [T1: Charaka Sutra 5/56-57] [single-source].
- Svarabheda is a listed nasya indication (Brimhana nasya for vataja voice loss); it is the vaidya's call when svarabheda persists after the urdhvaga movement has settled [T1: AH Sutra 20 Brimhana indications] [single-source].

### 4.3 Dhumapana (medicated smoke) — caution
- Charaka forbids dhumapana in **pitta vikara, raktapitta, ama**, after virechana/basti, in trishna, grief, fatigue, after milk/ghee/honey/curd, in timira, shirobhighata [T1: Charaka Sutra 5/41-45; AH Sutra 21] [verified x2].
- Rule for this corpus: no dhumapana during any Amlapitta phase with daha or ama; a vaidya may use mild snaihika dhuma later for a dry vataja hoarseness [T1: Charaka Sutra 5/41-45] [single-source for the applied rule].

### 4.4 Swarya dravyas and voice protection
- **The limit on cooling.** Charaka’s own gloss on the Kanthya dashemani states that kantha rogas are tridoshaja but **Vata-Kapha predominant**, and that vata-kapha-pacifying dravyas are therefore what benefit throat and voice. In Urdhvaga Amlapitta the underlying vitiation is Pitta, so the two rules must be held together: cool and sweeten the pitta, but do **not** let the regimen become ati-ruksha (excessively drying) or ati-sheeta (excessively cold), or the kantha itself will worsen — dry throat, voice fatigue, throat-clearing. The classical counterweight is snigdha: goghrita, Yashtimadhu ghrita, tila taila gandusha, milk-based kshirapaka, Draksha. Read every sheeta-ruksha choice in §2 and §3 (Triphala kwatha gandusha, Bhunimbadi and other tikta kwathas, long Guduchi or Kiratatikta courses) against this limit [T1: Charaka Sutra 4 (Kanthya dashemani, editors’ gloss on the group): "Disorders of throat are considered tridoshaja … however Vata and Kapha are predominant. Therefore Vata-Kapha pacifying drugs are beneficial in alleviating throat disorders and improving voice"; T1: Charaka Chikitsa 26/283-290 (vataja svarabheda is treated with ghrita and taila)] [verified x2].
- Kanthya mahakashaya (Charaka Sutra 4): Sariva, Ikshumula, Madhuka (Yashtimadhu), Pippali, Draksha, Vidari, **Kaitarya**, Hamsapadi, Brihati, Kantakarika — the tenth member is **Kaitarya**, read first-hand in the Sanskrit of Cha. Su. 4/9: "sārivekṣumūlamadhukapippalīdrākṣāvidārīkaiṭaryahaṁsapādībṛhatīkaṇṭakārikā iti daśemāni kaṇṭhyāni bhavanti" — these are the textual "throat-voice" herbs; Draksha, Ikshu, Vidari and Yashtimadhu address kantha-shosha, kantha-daha and vataja-pittaja-raktaja svarabheda [T1: Charaka Sutra 4/9 (Kanthya dashemani) — carakasamhitaonline Sanskrit and dashemani table; T3: IJCRT 2021 Kanthya mahakashaya review] [verified x2 — tenth member resolved 2026-09-18].
- Charaka’s agrya prakarana settles the head of the list: **Yashtimadhu is agrya (foremost) among kanthya dravyas**, and **unripe Kapittha (wood apple) is agrya among akanthya (throat-unwholesome) substances** — "āmaṁ kapittham akāṇṭhyānām" [T1: Charaka Sutra 25/40 agrya sangraha; corroborated first-hand by the file-03 validation pass] [verified x2]. Unripe kapittha therefore belongs on the apathya list for this patient alongside the amla-rasa nidanas.
- API karma "svarya": Draksha, Manjistha; "kanthya": Kantakari [T2: API monographs] [single-source each].
- Sheeta-svarya set for Urdhvaga Amlapitta (madhura-sheeta): Yashtimadhu, Draksha, Sariva, Vidari, Ikshu (sugarcane juice — Kashyapa's vamana medium too), Shatavari (API: svarabheda) [T1: Charaka Sutra 4; T2: API Satavari] [verified x2].
- Ushna-svarya set (kapha voice): Kantakari, Pippali, Brihati, Tulasi — only when the voice is heavy, wet and worse in the morning, never with daha [T1: Charaka Sutra 4; T2: API Kantakari, Tulasi] [verified x2].
- Yashtyadi basti (Charaka Siddhi 3/46) is explicitly indicated in **vaisvarya (hoarseness)**, vatarakta and visarpa — a classical link between a Yashtimadhu-milk basti and the voice (VAIDYA) [T1: Charaka Siddhi 3/46; T4: easyayurveda Yashtyadi vasti] [verified x2].
- Svarabheda chikitsa (Charaka Chikitsa 26/283-290, Trimarmiya): **vataja** — ghrita after food, and taila cooked with Bala, Rasna and Guduchi used four ways (pana, abhyanga, gandusha, basti), with mamsa rasa cooked with panchamula; **pittaja** — virechana first, then the sheeta-madhura recipes; **kaphaja** — strong nasya (shirovirechana), virechana, vamana and dhumapana, yava with katu dravyas, and a Pippali-Pathya or Chavya-Bharngi-Abhaya-Trikatu-Yavakshara-Chitraka lehya; **raktaja** — the paittika measures plus siravyadha; sannipatika — all of these except siravyadha [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 26/283-290, carakasamhitaonline, read first-hand] [verified x2]. Note for this corpus: the nasya of Charaka’s svarabheda chapter belongs to the **kaphaja** type; Brimhana nasya for vataja voice loss rests on AH Sutra 20.

### 4.5 Medicated ghee for the throat
- Yashtimadhu ghrita (AH Chikitsa 3): ½ tsp on the tongue, allowed to melt and trickle over the kantha before bed, on an empty stomach (not with active utklesha) [T1: AH Chikitsa 3; T4: ayurmedinfo] [verified x2].
- Jivantyadi ghrita: lineage use for dry voice; ¼ tsp before food [T4: ayurmedinfo Jivantyadi ghrita] [single-source].
- Plain goghrita 1 tsp with warm milk at night is Charaka's medhya-kanthya vehicle for Yashtimadhu churna (Charaka Chikitsa 1/3 medhya rasayana: Yashtimadhu churna with ksheera) [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 1/3] [single-source].
- Sequence rule: ghrita after the ama phase has passed (nirama: laghu koshtha, ruchi, no tikta-amla udgara); during ama, ghrita is apathya because it is guru and abhishyandi [T1: Kashyapa 16/20-21 (langhana and laghu bhojana first); Charaka Sutra 13 sneha rules] [single-source].

### 4.6 Sipping practices
- **Ushnodaka** (boiled, cooled-to-warm water) in small sips through the day is deepana-pachana and does not disturb pitta; sipping *during* meals is a Kashyapa nidana (water between morsels causes agnimandya) — keep water to small sips at mid-meal and none for an hour after [T1: Kashyapa Khila 16/6 — "antarōdakapānācca", within the 16/3-6 nidana list, read first-hand 2026-09-18; Sushruta Sutra 46 (madhya-bhojana ambupana)] [verified x2].
- **Dhanyaka hima** or **Dhanyaka-Jeeraka hima**: morning glass and daytime sipping for daha and trishna [T1: Sharangadhara Madhyama 4; T4: wisdomlib] [verified x2].
- **Ushira-siddha jala** (water kept over Ushira) as the summer sipping water in pitta [T2: API Usira (3-6 g for infusion)] [single-source].
- **Yashtimadhu phanta** (hot infusion, 1 g in 100 ml) sipped warm for a raw throat between meals (antarabhakta/muhurmuhu) [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 18/83 (Madhuka in the pittaja kasa vamana); Sharangadhara phanta kalpana] [single-source].
- **Ikshu rasa** (fresh sugarcane juice) is a Kashyapa vamana medium and a kanthya dravya — a small quantity in the morning is soothing, but it is guru-abhishyandi, so not in ama [T1: Kashyapa 16/31; Charaka Sutra 4 Kanthya] [verified x2].
- **Takra** (buttermilk) with roasted Jeeraka and a pinch of Saindhava at mid-day is the classical drink when kapha and ama are present; skip when daha is sharp. Read it accurately: Charaka calls takra **ushna-virya** and says it does not aggravate Pitta only by virtue of its **madhura vipaka** — so in an Urdhvaga flare with daha it is not a cooling drink, and the "heat-making" line of Sushruta Sutra 45 is said of **dadhi** (curd), not of takra [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 15/118 (takra ushna-virya, madhura-vipaka); Sushruta Sutra 45 (dadhi); Bhavaprakasha takra guna; corroborated first-hand by the file-03 validation pass; T4: easyayurveda Amlapitta home-remedies page] [verified x2].

### 4.7 Tambula (betel) caution
- Charaka lists tambula with Jatiphala, Lavanga, Kankola, Karpura for mouth freshness, and forbids it in **raktapitta, kshaya, pitta-prakriti, rukshata, murchha, madatyaya** and when the eyes are affected [T1: Charaka Sutra 5/76-77] [verified x2 with ayurvedamysore rendering].
- Nagavalli (betel leaf) is ushna-tikshna-katu; API lists svarabheda among its uses but in kapha type [T2: API Vol 3 Nagavalli] [single-source].
- Rule: no tambula, pan-masala, supari or chuna in Urdhvaga Amlapitta; the katu-ushna-lavana-kshara combination is a direct nidana for kantha-daha [T1: Charaka Sutra 5/76-77; Kashyapa 16/3-6] [single-source for the applied rule].

### 4.8 Kasa chikitsa principles for the kasa of Urdhvaga Amlapitta
- Charaka Chikitsa 18: kasa is five-fold (vataja, pittaja, kaphaja, kshataja, kshayaja); the first three are sadhya; the general order is nidana parivarjana, shodhana, shamana [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 18] [verified x2].
- **Pittaja kasa** (the Urdhvaga Amlapitta kasa with daha, tikta-asya, pita-nishthivana): vamana with ghrita or with Madana-Kashmari-Madhuka kwatha (Cha. Chi. 18/83), or with the klaka of Madanaphala and Madhuyashti in Vidari and Ikshurasa; then sheeta-madhura ahara; yogas of Yashtimadhu, Draksha, Sharkara with ghrita and honey; if sputum thin, Trivrit virechana with madhura dravyas then snigdha-sheeta; if thick, Trivrit with tikta then ruksha-sheeta [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 18 (pittaja kasa)] [verified x2].
- **Kaphaja kasa** (wet, heavy, morning cough with praseka): vamana if strong, then katu-ruksha-ushna ahara; Pippali with honey; Kantakari kwatha; Vasa svarasa with honey [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 18 (kaphaja kasa)] [verified x2].
- **Vataja-pittaja mixed (the dry hacking throat-clearing)**: snigdha-sheeta — Yashtimadhu ghrita, Eladi gutika, Draksha [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 18; Chikitsa 11 Eladi] [single-source].
- Untreated recurrent kasa progresses toward kshayaja kasa — the reason this kasa is treated at its dosha root, not suppressed at the throat [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 18 (progression)] [single-source].
- Practical sequencing for the Urdhvaga patient: first stop the upward pitta-kapha (langhana, tikta pachana, Kamadudha/Sutashekhara on prescription), then treat the cough as pittaja (Yashti-Draksha-Sharkara, Vasa) — Charaka's pittaja kasa yogas are all kantha-hitakara [T1: Kashyapa 16/18-22 sequence; Charaka Chikitsa 18] [verified x2].

## 5. Panchakarma for Amlapitta — all VAIDYA-supervised

Plain-language description of what the patient will experience; every step is decided and run by the vaidya.

### 5.1 Purva karma (preparation)
- **Deepana-pachana** (3-7 days): tikta-katu digestives until nirama signs appear (clear tongue, hunger, light abdomen, no sour belching) — Kashyapa's Nagara-Ativisha-Musta type or Chitrakadi vati (only in kapha) [T1: Kashyapa 16/24-25; Charaka Kalpa 1] [verified x2].
- **Snehapana** (internal oleation, 3-7 days): increasing morning doses of a chosen ghrita — Mahatiktaka ghrita or Shatavari ghrita for Amlapitta, plain goghrita in simple cases — until samyak snigdha signs (soft stool, oily skin, lightness, clarity) [T1: Charaka Sutra 13; T3: IJAM 2017 vamana case (ghrita snehapana)] [verified x2].
- **Abhyanga-svedana** (oil massage and mild sudation) for 1-3 days; svedana is mridu (gentle) in pitta [T1: Charaka Sutra 14 (sveda in pitta = mridu)] [single-source].
- Diet the evening before vamana: kapha-increasing (curd-rice, tila, masha) so kapha rises to the amashaya for easy expulsion [T1: Charaka Siddhi 1; T3: IJAM 2017] [verified x2].

### 5.2 Vamana (therapeutic emesis) — the pradhana karma for Urdhvaga
- Morning: Yashtimadhu phanta (hot infusion, 2-4 litres) or Kashyapa's media (salt water, warm milk, sugarcane juice, honey-water, bitters) drunk to the throat (akanthapana); then **Madanaphala-Pippali churna** (4-8 g) with Vacha (2-4 g), Saindhava (1-2 g) and honey; bouts (vega) are counted — 4 (hina), 6 (madhyama), 8 (pravara); ends when pitta appears after kapha and the patient feels light [T1: Charaka Kalpa 1; Charaka Siddhi 1; Kashyapa 16/31 (vamana media), read first-hand 2026-09-18; T3: IJAM 2017 vamana case study; Ayurlog 2021] [verified x2].
- Yashtimadhu phanta doubles as vamanopaga and pitta-shamaka, which is why it is the emetic medium of choice in Amlapitta rather than Nimba or lavana [T3: IJAM 2017; T1: Charaka Chikitsa 18 (Madhuka kwatha vamana in pittaja kasa)] [verified x2].
- Reported outcomes in Ayurvedic terms: relief of pitta-udirana, amlodgara, kantha-uro daha, avipaka by day 7, with samsarjana; long remission recorded [T3: IJAM 2017; researchgate 2021 case] [verified x2].
- Who is not fit (anarha): the very weak, emaciated, pregnant, the elderly, those with hridroga, urdhvaga raktapitta, or in wrong season — the vaidya screens [T1: Charaka Siddhi 2] [single-source].

### 5.3 Virechana (therapeutic purgation) — for Adhoga / pitta-dominant
- After snehapana-svedana, a single morning dose of the virechaka: Trivrit churna with Draksha kwatha (madhura anupana in pitta), Avipattikara churna 10-20 g (mridu, the commonest in Amlapitta), Aragvadha phala majja for tender koshtha, Eranda taila 15-30 ml when Vata obstructs; vegas counted (10/20/30) [T1: Charaka Kalpa 7 (Trivrit); Kashyapa 16/32-33 (Triphala-Trayamana-Katuka-Rohini each half pala with Trivrit at half that) and 16/33-37 (Patola patra and Triphala tvak half pala, Trayanti-Rohini-Nimba-Yashtika 1 karsha each, Masura 2 pala, cooked in 1 adhaka water to one-eighth, then with 1 kudava ghrita to half a prastha), read first-hand 2026-09-18; T4: panchakarma monographs] [verified x2].
- Kashyapa's masura-ghrita virechana yoga, drunk neither too cold nor too hot, purges easily and "removes even long-standing Amlapitta", and also quells vata-pitta, jvara, kushtha, visarpa, vatashonita, vidradhi, raktagulma and visphota [T1: Kashyapa Khila 16/33-37, read first-hand 2026-09-18] [verified x2].
- Kashyapa's rule: purge only when doshas are pakva and have descended (sramsana), not while they are upward and utklishta — otherwise vamana first [T1: Kashyapa 16/30 (sramsana of pakvashaya-gata doshas); 16/22 (no drava aushadhi while utklishta)] [verified x2].

### 5.4 Basti (medicated enema) — when Vata is involved
- Chakrapani, Vrinda Madhava and Govinda Das describe niruha basti for Amlapitta; Yogaratnakara also mentions raktamokshana [T1: Chakradatta / Vrinda Madhava / BR per IJNRD review] [single-source].
- **Yashtyadi (Yashtimadhu ksheera) basti** (**VAIDYA**) (Charaka Siddhi 3/46): milk cooked with Yashtimadhu 8 pala (384 g), honey 2 prasrita (192 g), ghrita 2 prasrita (192 g), Saindhava 1 karsha (12 g), kalka of Shatahva-Madanaphala-Pippali — "yaṣṭyāhvayasyāṣṭapalēna siddhaṁ payaḥ śatāhvāphalapippalībhiḥ | yuktaṁ sasarpirmadhu vātarakta-vaisvarya-vīsarpahitō nirūhaḥ" — indicated in vatarakta, **vaisvarya (hoarseness)**, visarpa; used in pitta-vata Amlapitta and post-shodhana [T1: Charaka Siddhi 3/46; T4: easyayurveda Yashtyadi vasti] [verified x2].
- **Piccha basti** (**VAIDYA**) (Charaka Siddhi 7): Shalmali niryasa mucilage with milk, ghee, honey (some versions Yashtimadhu-tila) — for pravahika, raktatisara; applied by vaidyas in Adhoga Amlapitta with vidbheda and daha [T1: Charaka Siddhi 7; T4: easyayurveda] [single-source for the Amlapitta use].
- **Dhanyaka hima basti** (**VAIDYA**) — reported for Amlapitta with urdhvaga (upward-moving) features [T3: researchgate 2024 case report] [single-source].
- **Matra basti** (**VAIDYA**) with Shatavari/Yashtimadhu ghrita or taila 60 ml after food for evening Vata-type shula and udgara [T1: Charaka Siddhi 4 (matra basti)] [single-source].

### 5.5 Nasya, Shirodhara, Raktamokshana
- Nasya (marsha dose **VAIDYA**; pratimarsha is the self-use dose): only in the nirama phase (see §4.2); Brimhana nasya for persisting vataja svarabheda; Anu taila pratimarsha in season [T1: AH Sutra 20; Charaka Sutra 5] [verified x2].
- Shirodhara (**VAIDYA**; oil or takra stream on the forehead) for the manasika component — chinta, shoka, bhaya, krodha — with Ksheerabala taila or Brahmi taila; 7-14 days [T1: AH Sutra 22 (murdha taila: dhara); T3: IJMHS 2022, which attributes a manasika hetu to Kashyapa] [**unverified 2026-09-18**: the Khila 16/3-6 nidana list was read first-hand and is entirely aharaja-viharaja — chinta, shoka, bhaya and krodha do **not** appear in it; the manasika reading is the reviewers’, not Kashyapa’s wording. The therapy itself stands on the general manas-vikara rule, not on this chapter.]
- Raktamokshana (**VAIDYA**; bloodletting) is mentioned by Yogaratnakara for Amlapitta; rare, for pitta-rakta with kandu-mandala-pidaka on the skin [T1: Yogaratnakara Amlapitta per IJNRD] [single-source].

### 5.6 Paschat karma — samsarjana krama in plain terms
- After vamana or virechana the agni is like a small flame; food is rebuilt over 3, 5 or 7 days for hina, madhyama, pravara shuddhi: peya (thin rice gruel) → vilepi (thick gruel) → akrita yusha (plain mung soup) → krita yusha (seasoned) → akrita mamsa rasa/ krita → normal diet [T1: Charaka Siddhi 1/11-14 (peyadi krama)] [verified x2 with JAIMS review].
- During samsarjana: no ghee-heavy, sour, salty, fermented or cold foods; small warm meals; rest; no exertion, no day-sleep, no loud speaking (voice rest belongs here too) [T1: Charaka Siddhi 1 (pariharya after shodhana)] [single-source].
- Shamana yogas resume after samsarjana; rasayana begins only after full shuddhi and agni recovery [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 1/1/24-28] [verified x2].

## 6. Rasayana for jirna Amlapitta and after shodhana

- Eligibility: rasayana works after snehana-svedana and shodhana have cleaned the body, "as dye takes on a clean cloth"; in a body with doshas it fails [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 1/1/24-28] [verified x2].
- **Amalaki rasayana** — the Amlapitta rasayana: sheeta, madhura-vipaka, tridoshajit; 3-6 g bhavita churna with ghee-honey or milk each morning for 45 days to 3 months [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 1/1/75, 1/3; T2: API Amalaki (Amlapitta, Daha)] [verified x2].
- **Shatavari rasayana**: 3-6 g with milk at night; API: Amlapitta, parinama shula, svarabheda [T2: API Satavari; T1: BP Nighantu] [verified x2].
- **Yashtimadhu rasayana** (medhya): Yashtimadhu churna 2-4 g with milk; Charaka names it among the four medhya rasayanas; kanthya and svarya [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 1/3 (Yashtimadhu churna with ksheera)] [verified x2].
- **Guduchi rasayana**: Guduchi svarasa (medhya) or satva 500 mg-1 g; suited when jvara-daha-aruchi linger [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 1/3 (Guduchi svarasa medhya); T4: ayurmedinfo Guduchi satva] [verified x2].
- **Chyawanprash**: for the kasa-svarabheda-kshaya tail of jirna Amlapitta once daha has settled; 5-10 g morning with warm milk after; not during ama, aruchi or active daha because of honey-ghee-spice bulk [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 1/1/62-74] [verified x2].
- **Brahma rasayana**: 5-10 g morning with milk, after full shodhana, for the depleted patient with manasika involvement [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 1/1/41-57] [single-source].
- **Kushmanda/Khandamalaki rasayana**: brimhana-sheeta confection when weight and voice strength are low [T1: Chakradatta; BR Raktapitta 45-51] [verified x2].
- **Pippali rasayana (vardhamana)**: Charaka's kasa-shvasa rasayana; but continuous Pippali increases kapha-pitta (Charaka Vimana 1/16) — in Amlapitta only under a vaidya, short, with honey-ghee [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 1/3; Charaka Vimana 1/16] [verified x2].
- **Achara rasayana** (behavioural rasayana, Charaka Chikitsa 1/4/30-35): satya (truthful speech), akrodha (no anger), nivritti from madya and maithuna excess, ahimsa, anayasa (no over-exertion), prashanta (calm), priyavadi (pleasant speech), japa, shaucha, dhairya, dana, tapas, sama-jagarana-svapna (balanced waking and sleep), regular milk and ghee, respect for elders and the learned, adhyatma — "one who lives so obtains every fruit of rasayana without a drug". Kashyapa's closing line on Amlapitta says the same: yukta ahara-vihara, befitting vyayama, alola (free of tongue-greed), atmavan (self-commanded), sat (a good man) [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 1/4/30-35; Kashyapa Khila 16/42, read first-hand 2026-09-18] [verified x2].
- Voice-specific achara: priyavadi and mita-bhashana (measured speech) are rasayana behaviours that also rest the svara [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 1/4/30-35] [single-source for the voice inference].

## 7. Safety in Ayurvedic terms

### 7.1 Matra (dose) and satmya
- The API metric doses are conversions of classical weights (1 karsha = 12 g churna; kwatha 2 pala); a yoga's dose is decided by agni, bala, vaya, desha, kala and satmya, and the patient begins at the lower end [T1: Sharangadhara Purva 1 (mana), Madhyama 2; Charaka Vimana 8 (dashavidha pariksha)] [verified x2].
- Sudden change of habituated food or medicine is asatmya; new dravyas are introduced by the padamshika krama (quarter-steps) [T1: Charaka Sutra 7/36-37] [single-source].
- Duration: most churnas and kwathas run 2-8 weeks; Avipattikara 1-2 months then pause; asavas ≤ 3-4 months; bhasmas and rasaushadhi only for the vaidya's stated course [T4: easyayurveda/ayurmedinfo monographs] [verified x2 across monographs].

### 7.2 Ushna dravyas that aggravate Pitta in Amlapitta
Counterpart caution: kantha rogas are Vata-Kapha predominant (§4.4), so the opposite error — an ati-ruksha or ati-sheeta regimen — is equally real in this patient. Ruksha-tikta kwathas on long courses, repeated kashaya gandusha and cold sipping without any snigdha counterweight produce kantha-shosha, voice fatigue and more throat-clearing. Pair them with goghrita, Yashtimadhu ghrita, tila taila gandusha or a milk kshirapaka [T1: Charaka Sutra 4 (kanthya group gloss); Sushruta Chikitsa 40/44-45 (atiyoga of gargling: trishna, mukha-shosha, aruchi, klama)] [verified x2].

### 7.4 Yashtimadhu long-term guidance (classical and API)
- Charaka prescribes Yashtimadhu churna with milk as a medhya rasayana, i.e., for sustained use in the fit person; the API dose is 2-4 g/day of churna; guru-snigdha-madhura means it can raise kapha in kapha prakriti or with ama — pause when praseka, gaurava or aruchi appear [T1: Charaka Chikitsa 1/3; T2: API Vol 1; T4: easyayurveda] [verified x2].
- Monographs advise courses of a few weeks and review; larger daily amounts are outside matra — discuss with your vaidya before exceeding 4 g/day or 6-8 weeks continuous [T4: easyayurveda licorice page] [single-source].

### 7.5 Rasaushadhi purity
- A bhasma is valid only after shodhana (purification) and marana (incineration) tests: varitara (floats on water), rekhapurna (enters finger-lines), nishchandra (no metallic lustre), apunarbhava (does not revert), nirdhuma; Shankha bhasma needs two gajaputa cycles; Pravala pishti needs the rose-water trituration [T1: Rasa Tarangini 2 and 12/17-18; Rasa Ratna Samuccaya 8] [verified x2].
- Take rasaushadhi only as dispensed by the vaidya from a classical pharmacy (Kottakkal AVS, AVP, SDM, Baidyanath, Zandu, Dabur) with batch identity; never self-source Parada-Gandhaka or Vatsanabha yogas [T1: Rasa Tarangini (shodhana necessity); T4: pharmacy monographs] [verified x2].

### 7.6 Viruddha (incompatible) combinations relevant to the medicines
- Honey and ghee in equal quantity by weight are matra-viruddha; every "madhu-ghrita anupana" above is unequal [T1: Charaka Sutra 26/81-84] [verified x2].
- Honey never heated or with hot drinks; take honey anupana with warm, not hot, water [T1: Charaka Sutra 26 (ushna-viruddha of madhu)] [single-source].
- Milk with amla-lavana (sour-salt) is samyoga-viruddha — Shatavari/Yashtimadhu kshirapaka at night should not follow a sour or salty snack; asavas and milk-based yogas are separated by two hours [T1: Charaka Sutra 26/81-84] [single-source].
- Kulattha, tila, masha, dadhi at night, and apakva madya are Amlapitta nidanas, not only apathya — stop them before judging a medicine's effect [T1: Kashyapa 16/3-6 (kulattha, pishtanna, apakva madya, gorasa, guru-abhishyandi, paryushita, adhyashana all stand in the list), read first-hand 2026-09-18; Charaka Sutra 25 (kulattha agrya for Amlapitta)] [verified x2].
